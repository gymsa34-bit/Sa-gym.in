About SA GYM

A gym built for serious results.

SA GYM is Dhanbad's premium fitness club at Bank More. We combine imported equipment, clean private amenities and hands-on coaching so every member trains comfortably and progresses faster.

🏋️Imported Machines

Plate-loaded and selectorised lines built for form and safety.

🧖Steam & Recovery

Private steam and shower suites to recover after training.

🧘Yoga & Zumba

Instructor-led classes in a dedicated mirrored studio.

🔒Private Lockers

Secure personal lockers and individual change rooms.

World-class amenities

Facilities, engineered for results.

Every corner is built for a complete training experience: strength, cardio, recovery and comfort.

01

Free Weights Zone

Full dumbbell rack up to heavy weights, adjustable benches and cable stations along a full mirror wall.

02

Strength Floor

Imported plate-loaded and selectorised machines, dumbbells to heavy racks, all on a premium wooden floor.

03

Leg Press & Plate-Loaded

Commercial leg press, lat pulldown and bench stations for serious lower-body and back training.

04

Cardio Zone

A dedicated section of treadmills, spin bikes and stair climbers, separated from the weights.

05

Cross-Trainers

Low-impact elliptical stations and treadmills with live performance consoles.

06

Stair Climber

Commercial stepmill for serious conditioning and glute-leg endurance.

07

Yoga, Zumba & Functional

Mirrored studio with battle ropes, a punching dummy and certified instructors.

08

Functional & Aerobics Corner

Step platforms, mats, swiss ball, kettlebells and medicine balls for functional and group training.

09

Steam & Shower

Private steam and shower suites for recovery after every session.

10

Personal Change Rooms

Private change rooms with full-length mirror, fan and clean finish.

11

Private Lockers

Secure personal lockers so your belongings stay safe while you train.

12

Dumbbell & Bench Area

Rubber-coated dumbbells and flat, incline and adjustable benches, ready for every split.

Questions

Frequently asked.

What are the membership plans and prices?

1 Month ₹2,500, 3 Months ₹5,000, 6 Months ₹8,500 and 1 Year ₹14,000. All prices include GST with no hidden charges. Personal training and couple plans are available on request.

What facilities are included?

Full workout floors with imported machines, a separate cardio zone, steam bath, private lockers, personal change rooms, separate washrooms and instructor-led Yoga & Zumba classes (from the 3-month plan).

Are Yoga & Zumba classes taught by an instructor?

Yes. Classes are led by a certified instructor in a dedicated mirrored studio. Timings are available at the helpdesk.

What are the gym timings?

Monday to Saturday, 6:00 AM to 9:30 PM. Sunday closed.

Where is SA GYM located?

Our branch is at Goodwill Property, Ground Floor, Opposite Centre Point, Bank More, Dhanbad.

Do you sell gym equipment?

Yes. Visit our equipment store section for benches and machines, then enquire on WhatsApp for the final price.
